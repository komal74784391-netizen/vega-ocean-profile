# Marisol Vega

## About

Marisol Vega is a PhD candidate in Computational Oceanography at Kestrel Bay University.

Current affiliation: Kestrel Bay University (KBU).

Prior institution: Halcyon Institute for Coastal Studies.

Degree: MSc in Applied Mathematics (Halcyon Institute for Coastal Studies, 2019).
